## 3.1 Research approach

This study uses a **mixed-method design**. A quantitative online survey tests hypotheses H1–H4 and compares guest groups (SQ1, SQ2). Semi-structured interviews in FRP 2.2 explain the survey results and explore which interventions hotels can realistically implement (SQ3).

## 3.2 Sample

**Survey.** Adults (18+) who stayed in a hotel in the Netherlands in the past 12 months. We use convenience and snowball sampling with a screening question. The target sample size is to be agreed with our coach.

**Interviews.** Hotel managers, sustainability staff and front-office staff, selected through purposive sampling, until data saturation is reached.

## 3.5 Data analysis

Survey data will be analysed in **SPSS**:

- descriptive statistics (means and standard deviations per group);
- correlation and multiple linear regression to test H1–H4;
- independent samples t-test and one-way ANOVA for differences between guest groups (SQ2).

Interviews will be coded in **ATLAS.ti**, working from codes to themes.

## 3.6 Reliability and validity

Reliability is checked with Cronbach's alpha per construct, after recoding the negatively worded items. Validity is supported by basing the items on published studies and by a pilot test to check that respondents understand the questions.

## 3.7 Ethical considerations

Participation is voluntary and anonymous, with informed consent. Interviewees may use a different name. Recordings and transcripts are stored securely and used only for this project.
